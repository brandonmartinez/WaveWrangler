# M4 kickoff policy seed (not yet an executable kickoff)

M4 does **not** start from this draft. At the reviewed M3 exit, replace this seed with a standalone copyable kickoff containing the actual final main SHA, accepted and failed M3 gates, current live M4 issues, required consent, and the M4 exit proof. Do not imply that a proposed M3 result passed.

## Coordination rules to carry into the completed kickoff

- Follow the canonical **actual-diff model/effort tiers** in `.squad/routing.md` and the resolver in `.squad/templates/model-selection-reference.md` on every new session or task spawn. Pass both parameters explicitly: high GPT only for changed safety invariants/complex DSP and their safety review; mid GPT for routine UI/integration/docs/config/test harness; fast GPT or direct shell for deterministic tests/builds/log parsing and bounded triage. Same-tier GPT fallback only; Claude requires a recorded exception. Do not auto-upgrade for coordination, images, reviewer title or prior rejection, and do not hot-switch ongoing work.
- Use one fresh isolated session per PR/unit, **not** per minor command. A kickoff carries objective, owned paths, relevant invariant/permission decisions, dependencies, acceptance commands and a bounded stop; never paste all charters, decisions, histories or raw logs. Normally cap at **four** genuinely independent writers plus coordinator and one independent reviewer; M3's temporary six-writer evening exception does not carry into M4.
- Omit `notify_on_idle` for children that send an explicit handoff/needs_input/error; use `"once"` only when it is the sole completion signal, never `"always"`. One substantive completion message, no duplicate acknowledgments or polling turns. Sweep finished children on substantive completed-unit handoffs, about every 2 h of active work and at the final handoff; verify merged/closed/superseded work, no open PR/automation, clean pushed worktree and copied evidence before archival. Existing app notification settings are not retroactively changed without an API.
- Use bounded shell/helper processes or one fast runner for deterministic work, with exact SHA, selectors/timeouts and one terminal result. Reuse safe warm build and exact-SHA GUI Products during iteration; the existing **fresh exact-code-head full `scripts/test.sh` pre-merge gate** and applicable GUI/safety gates remain mandatory. Docs/Squad-only changes retain their documented exemption.

This seed changes orchestration cost, not original-source immutability, honest recovery, offline privacy, protected speech, human acceptance or essential accessibility.
