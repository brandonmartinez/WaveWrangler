## Product intent
WaveWrangler is a native macOS preparation workspace with local audio processing, referenced source media, and cloud-hosted canonical project documents in MVP.
Organize recordings, establish synchronization across recorder groups, review conservative speech edits, and deliver synchronized, editable material to a DAW.
The DAW remains responsible for mixing, EQ, compression, effects, and mastering.
An Apple Design Award is an aspiration, not a requirement, certification, or promised outcome.
Differentiation is a hypothesis: project organization plus clock-aware preparation plus cautious, reconstructible edits may reduce handoff friction.
Competitor documentation demonstrates overlapping capabilities; it does not establish a unique market gap. [L1–L10](research.md#competitor-evidence)

## People and jobs
- Podcast producer: keep many projects and episodes findable, resume unfinished work, and hand off a predictable session.
- Recording editor: correct messy file/group/speaker assignments, synchronize independent recorders, and inspect uncertainty.
- Host or solo producer: accept, reject, adjust, audition, and undo contextual filler proposals without losing other speech.
- DAW engineer: receive common-origin tracks and editable provenance, restore removed material, then mix/master elsewhere.
- Keyboard and VoiceOver user: complete these same tasks without dragging, waveform-only information, or color-only status.

## Accepted organization and candidate information model
- **Library:** durable organization of projects, collections, recent/unavailable entries, and user metadata; never merely a disposable cache.
- **Project:** one durable project per podcast/show, containing multiple episodes and their assets, references, and decisions; cloud canonical document locations are in MVP.
- **Episode:** a recording/edit/export unit with its own groups, speaker assignments, accepted alignment, and edit revision.
- **Recorder group:** sources known to share a capture clock; individual clips may have different starts or capture epochs.
- **Clip/channel:** immutable source reference plus placement/channel assignment; files, channels, and speakers are not interchangeable.
- **Speaker:** a user-confirmed identity with selected primary channel/clip assignments and retained backups; unknown is valid.
- Shallow, initially visible project sidebar; deeper episode/group/channel hierarchy belongs in episode content, not nested sidebar trees.
- Proposed destinations are Setup, Alignment, Review, and Export: revisitable workspaces, not a one-way wizard; blocked destinations expose a reason and remedy. [Information architecture](research.md#information-architecture) · [D3–D4](research.md#design-evidence)

## Core workflow
1. Create/open a show project, select an episode, and save/resume through native macOS document conventions, including user-chosen cloud locations.
2. Select referenced sources/folders with explicit permission. Automatic source downloads are configurable and **ON by default**, with accessible status and an off setting; metadata-only/off paths do not silently read, hash, preview, or decode content.
3. Confirm/correct proposed groups, clip epochs/starts, channels, speakers, and primaries/backups; never trust filenames as identity.
4. Resolve denied, unavailable, changed, or unverified references through explicit regrant/relink/make-available actions.
5. After content-processing consent, align groups, examine offsets/drift/coverage, audition, and correct uncertain anchors manually.
6. Analyze only confirmed primaries locally; inspect transcript context and validated word timing, not blanket filler-token deletion.
7. Review each proposal with linked transcript/list/timeline, multi-speaker preview, boundaries, accept/reject, editable per-cut shortening/lift modes, fades, and named undo; no automatic filler acceptance.
8. Export zero-origin cleaned speaker WAV stems plus a documented editable reconstructive cut record and explicit source/recipe/asset requirements; demonstrate restoration/regeneration when those assets are available, without requiring a native DAW session.

## Baseline edit semantics
- Accepted exact default: **“Shorten when safe and by default, but allow the user to edit that point and choose a different method”** (Brandon, Q05, 2026-10-04).
- Every human-accepted shortening uses **one common episode edit map on every previewed/exported track**. Per-cut modes and boundaries are editable, reversible, and versioned.
- **Timing-preserving speaker lift** is an alternative, especially when another speaker overlaps; it removes only safe target material without collapsing episode time.
- Protect other-primary speech and intelligible crosstalk on the target stem; an empty transcript is not proof of silence.
- Uncertain timing, overlap, or semantic classification causes abstention or manual review, never silent destructive acceptance.
- A contextual “um” or “like” may be meaningful; show surrounding speech, rationale, and evidence, then require human acceptance.
- Fades are short, adjustable, previewed, versioned decisions; preserve meaningful onsets/tails and supported multi-speaker overlap.
- Overrides never waive protected, meaningful, or other speech; unsafe shortening offers a safe lift, manual/review/abstain, not forced collapse.
- Never shorten one speaker independently or delete another speaker's overlapping speech. [Edit contract](research.md#edit-contract)

## Timing, assets, and handoff guardrails
- Distinguish source frames, clip/group epochs, aligned episode time, edited time, and export frames; record every transform/version.
- Shared clock does not imply shared starts; timestamps alone do not prove oscillator locking.
- Correlation confidence is evidence, not probability until calibrated; unsupported/disconnected regions remain manual or blocked.
- Constant/variable acoustic-delay negatives are scored against independent clock truth, not correlation-peak agreement.
- Positive, held-out-validated maps only; detect capture restarts/gaps and never smooth over discontinuities silently.
- Clock-rate resampling and pitch-preserving stretching are distinct; phase/listening quality must be measured, not assumed.
- All same-group channels share maps and processing; analysis downmix/gain never leaks into renders.
- Provisional skew/polarity/leakage budgets, fixture-specific phase tolerances, and listening criteria must be calibrated/frozen before holdout. [Render contract](research.md#rendering-and-channel-integrity)
- One cleaned stem per speaker shares zero origin, common time map/rate/duration, silence padding and actual rounded integer boundaries; retain uncut aligned media or demonstrably reconstructible equivalents and complete asset requirements, without forcing portable copies.
- Stems alone are not reversible. A human- and machine-editable manifest records actual rounded frame boundaries plus source/maps/model/edit/assets/renderer versions, cuts, fades, gains, rates, and offsets.
- Export uses a frozen validated revision snapshot; concurrent editing cannot mix revisions, and completion identifies the exported revision even when current work has advanced.
- Bake versus editable fades/gains is explicit; never apply the same processing twice. [Alignment](research.md#alignment-contract) · [Export](research.md#export-contract)
- Default export is **48 kHz/24-bit PCM WAV**, configurable. Offer feasible detected source settings, preferring 48 kHz; mixed input rates require one coherent common output rate. “cross compatible with any DAW” is the user's goal, not verified universal WAV compatibility; no required REAPER/native-session gate.

## Feasibility options and non-goals
- Accepted evaluation direction: **macOS 26+, Apple silicon only, English only**; Intel and non-English languages deferred. Initial performance reference is **16 GB**, not a proven minimum; 8 GB evaluation is later.
- SpeechTranscriber is eligible, not chosen or validated. Compare native Speech fairly with local Whisper (whisper.cpp/WhisperKit) on timing, offline behavior, asset provisioning/downloads and updates; preserve Apple device/locale/entitlement/pinning unknowns and WhisperKit's pinned floor-resolution gate.
- Each exact model download needs separate approval **after source, license and size are known**. Native asset provisioning/network consent is separate; source-download defaults never authorize model downloads. Code/model/transitive/redistribution/patent rights remain artifact-specific.
- **Signed/notarized direct download first**; App Store first launch deferred. Signing, hardened runtime, permissions/privacy and redistribution clearance remain gates; no paid/legal budget or license assurance is approved.
- Common macOS import formats are MVP subject to validation. WAV/BWF, AIFF, CAF, AAC/M4A, MP3 and optional supported FLAC are candidates, not a promised list. Validate priming/padding/time mapping, variable rates, bit depth, channels, corrupt/unsupported errors and decoder build licenses; prefer native decoding only if evidence supports it.
- No full mixing/mastering, automatic silence/pause cleanup, real-time AU/VST, custom training, or mandatory diarization.
- No unvalidated AAF/OMF promise, guaranteed cloud-provider classifier, default portable copies, or automatic bulk filler deletion.
- “Offline” excludes authorized provisioning; runtime fallback to cloud speech is prohibited. [Options and clearance](research.md#options-and-clearance)
