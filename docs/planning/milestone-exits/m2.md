## 6. Supported envelope and limits

[WW-050's synthetic, native decoder envelope](../../m2/evidence/ww-050-decode-envelope.md) supports WAVE/AIFF/AIFC/CAF PCM with evidenced 16/24/32-bit integer and 32/64-bit float variants on the documented rate grid (8–192 kHz, 1–8 channels where listed); M4A/CAF ALAC and FLAC at evidenced lossless depths/rates and 1/2/6/8 channels; M4A/CAF AAC at 8–48 kHz and 1/2/6/8 channels; CAF Opus at 8/16/24/48 kHz, 1–2 channels. Exact per-container endian, bit-depth and fixture restrictions are in the linked matrix, **not** a promise of every Cartesian combination. Lossless supported cases are bit-exact; lossy landmarks are within the stated output-frame limit after priming/padding. Output settings choose a common feasible rate and retain channels without up/downmix. Refused: MP3, raw/ADTS AAC, RF64/W64/BWF extensions, µ-law/A-law/IMA4, 8-bit/packed 20-bit PCM, unlisted rates/layouts/codecs, unverifiable streaming length, bad/changed/unavailable sources; payload bit flips accepted by a codec cannot always be detected. Real cloud/dataless/provider decoding and reference-device compatibility are unproven.

The estimator **abstains on the approved real multi-mic material**; [#188](https://github.com/brandonmartinez/WaveWrangler/issues/188) carries its eligible-window/primary-speech diagnosis to M4. Numeric/anchor manual correction is available without a clock-truth claim; no `clockApproved` construction path exists. The rendered full-length **synthetic** six-channel 75-minute case took 375.145 s with peak 745 MiB RSS and 709 MiB footprint at 180-second segments; the exploratory 300-second setting reached **1,042 MiB**, beyond the 1 GiB family gate, hence [#235](https://github.com/brandonmartinez/WaveWrangler/issues/235) (M3). The 36-second real-media copied render is not a full-length real-media memory qualification. Output is an internal, versioned, cancellable derived asset; **no M2 export, cleaned stems, transcription or release claim**.
